Meri Jaisi Proof Layer

Creator demos become suitability-matched proof on the Meesho PDP, matched on skin tone, skin type or hair type, and paid only on delivered conversions.

Archit Singla · Abhinav Sagar · Meesho DICE Challenge S3 · Business Track · semi-final prototype

Source on GitHub

Design mockup in Meesho's visual language, not the live app. Creators, counts, prices, views and payouts are illustrative; creator portraits and demo frames are AI-generated images of fictional people. The logic is the one described in the deck. Nothing you do here leaves your browser.

Where each screen comes from in the deck
Slide 5Tag, Attach, Match, Pay: the Creator tab (tag and attach), the Shopper tab (match, on one of three axes: skin tone, skin type or hair type) and the Ledger tab (pay, with the attribution rules).
Slide 6The PDP: chip A on the first image, match module B at the shade selector, review filter C, 3-tap quiz D, and the tier-wise capture table on the right of the Shopper tab. The phone mock follows Meesho's live Android PDP as of October 2026: delivery banner, trust strip and a single Buy button.
Slide 7Bottlenecks and mitigations: payout throttle (Ledger), honest no-demo state and Pareto coverage (Brand), neutral tone scale with no label shown (quiz), lite media modes (Tier 3 and 4).
Slide 8Three coverage tiers A, B and C as the BPC products you can switch between, with the Anti-Frizz Shampoo as the hair-type case; the Demo-Wanted board; the return-rate circuit breaker on inherited demos.
Slide 9The five gates, traced live on every PDP open; the Never and Fallback rules; the Puchho assistant grounded in demos, reviews and ingredients.
Slide 10Everything here is the v0 scope: a PDP widget, a tagging sheet and a manual payout run, which is why each screen is simple enough to run from a sheet before any engineering.
