Meri Jaisi Proof Layer

Interactive prototype of the Round 2 deck for the Meesho DICE Challenge, Season 3, Business Track. Archit Singla and Abhinav Sagar.

Design mockup in Meesho's visual language, not the live app. Creators, counts, prices, views and payouts are illustrative; the logic is the one described in the deck. Nothing you do here leaves your browser.

Where each screen comes from in the deck
Slide 5Tag, Attach, Match, Pay: the Creator tab (tag and attach), the Shopper tab (match) and the Ledger tab (pay, with the attribution rules).
Slide 6The PDP: chip A on the first image, match module B at the shade selector, review filter C, 3-tap quiz D, and the tier-wise capture table on the right of the Shopper tab.
Slide 7Bottlenecks and mitigations: payout throttle (Ledger), honest no-demo state and Pareto coverage (Brand), neutral tone scale with no label shown (quiz), lite media modes (Tier 3 and 4).
Slide 8Three coverage tiers A, B and C as the three BPC products you can switch between; the Demo-Wanted board; the return-rate circuit breaker on inherited demos.
Slide 9The five gates, traced live on every PDP open; the Never and Fallback rules; the Puchho assistant grounded in demos, reviews and ingredients.
Slide 10Everything here is the v0 scope: a PDP widget, a tagging sheet and a manual payout run, which is why each screen is simple enough to run from a sheet before any engineering.
